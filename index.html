This is a box of keepsakes that you open to hear. I took a box of small things I’ve collected over the past 4 years that mean something to me, a ticket, a note, a lanyard, a few cards and rebuilt it as a 3D object you can look into. Each object inside carries a sound I recorded that is connected to its object. The sound isn’t the noise the object itself would make; it’s the memory behind it. When you rest the pointer on any object, it lifts up a little and the sound of its memory plays, then repeats quieter and fades away, the way a memory does. The whole piece is meant to feel quiet and personal, like opening a drawer in someone’s room.

Hover over the objects after opening the box to listen to their connected sounds.

  • Turtley amazing cardA hand-painted card given by my friend, and everyone was laughing on it.
  • Birthday cardMy most recent birthday, when everyone wrote me a note on these small cards.
  • Handwritten noteAn autograph from an artist called Darzi. The audio is from the gig.
  • Album coverThe artwork from The Strokes’ album cover. It plays a clip of one of the songs.
  • Train ticketA paper ticket from a Mumbai train. It plays a sound of me and my friends walking towards the station.
  • Serendipity lanyardA crumpled checkered ribbon from the Serendipity Arts Festival. It plays a sound from that time.
  • Subko Cacao packetA bar of milk chocolate in a teal wrapper. It plays the sounds of the day we got the chocolate in Mumbai with my friends.
  • 3D glassesColdplay paper glasses with “look with love” written on them. They play a Coldplay track from their concert day in Ahmedabad.
  • Cat stickerA hand-drawn sleeping cat, one of the cats I used to feed. It plays the cat’s sound.
  • Bouquet and polaroidsA kraft-paper cone of dried flowers with four polaroids tucked in. It plays the audio from the day I got it for my friend.
